The Northern Agricultural and Horticultural Society runs the two-day North Show and Battle of Flowers at Saumarez Park each August. Entries are invited in livestock, horticulture, floral art, home industries and baking. The society promotes horticulture in the Castel, Vale and St Sampson parishes.
