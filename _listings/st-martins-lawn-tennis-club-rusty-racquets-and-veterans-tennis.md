Members' tennis club in St Martin with five all-weather courts, three floodlit, running regular social tennis and summer tournaments. Its Rusty Racquets sessions run every Saturday for all levels, and the 2026 calendar includes an over-65s veterans event. A low-cost social membership is offered to new members.
