All Guernsey swimmers, whether you swim all year round or want encouragement to swim in the winter for the first time, are welcome to join the group. Here you can find a swim buddy or just share fantastic moments of your swim. Swimming all year is great for mental health; it's a feeling you cannot explain. You need to experience it for yourself.
