Community classes and courses are in place to support those who are struggling to get more involved in the community. We want these to be safe places where those who need that extra help, are able to attend and enjoy an activity that may help to improve their health and wellbeing in a fun and relaxed way. These sessions are held at Styx Centre and KGV Community Centre.
