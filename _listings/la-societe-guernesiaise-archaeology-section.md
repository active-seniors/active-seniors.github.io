This section of La Société Guernesiaise works with Guernsey Museum on fieldwork, site recording, geophysical surveys and monitoring coastal prehistoric sites across the Bailiwick. Volunteers can help with digging and washing finds, or attend site visits, lectures and workshops. Section membership is free.
