Held every Tuesday from 1pm to 2pm at Beau Sejour, the group plays all kinds of adapted sports such as floor curling, boccia, football, tennis, bowling, table tennis and other games. Adults with a range of disabilities and health conditions are welcome; please come with a carer if you need one. For more information contact Nicky Will at the Sports Commission.
