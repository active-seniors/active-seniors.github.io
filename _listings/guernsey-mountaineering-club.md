The GMC mainly participates in rock climbing and rock climbing-related activities on the Island of Guernsey in the Channel Islands (Small group of islands between England and France). It’s members also participate in other mountaineering-related activities whenever they get the chance. To check up to date schedule, please visit our online calendar or for more information contact us via e-mail or online form.
