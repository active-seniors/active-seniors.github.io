Relaxed, sociable life drawing for all skill levels, with everything provided. Sessions start at 7pm sharp, so arrive from 6.40pm for the comfiest seats. There's time to chat before the start and in the 20-minute break, and a strong, welcoming community of artists aged from 18 to 80-plus.
