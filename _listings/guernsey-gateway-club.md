The club organises social and leisure activities for adults with a learning disability in Guernsey. There are about 45 members at any one time. Anyone with a learning disability, aged eighteen or older and living in Guernsey, may apply to attend. Activities include bar meals, bingo, beetle drives, disco's, etc.
