A non-contact version of rugby played at walking pace, with no running, tackling or heavy impacts. The focus is on passing, teamwork and tactics in a relaxed, social setting. Run by Guernsey Raiders, it is open to all abilities and to people with no rugby experience; players must be over 18. Email the club's development team or join its WhatsApp group for session details.
