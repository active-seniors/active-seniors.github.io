The Yoga Project emerged out of Michael Wegerer’s enthusiasm for Yoga. Michael's yoga classes and his one-to-one lessons are taught in a way that are suitable for anyone, no matter what their level of fitness or capability. His classes are organised around themes that extend over several weeks. This allows participants to develop a deep personal understanding and experience of yoga.
