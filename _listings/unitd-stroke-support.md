Every Thursday at Unltd we run Communication Club 2-3.30 where we provide support for stroke survivors with aphasia (communication difficulties). Loved ones and carers are welcome. This is a free event and no booking is required. Third Wednesday of each month in Le Marchant Ward dining room, Princess Elizabeth Hospital 3-4.30 we run our Stroke Cafe. An informal place for people affected by stroke to meet others in a similar situation and socialise in a relaxed setting
