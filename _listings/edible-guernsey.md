Edible Guernsey is a community of growers, gardeners and food enthusiasts working together to create a more sustainable and resilient local food system. Through shared learning, practical projects and community engagement, it helps people of all ages grow food, develop new skills and connect with their community. Experienced growers and beginners are both welcome. It offers a two-tier membership for individuals and organisations, with details on application, and visits to the growing site can be arranged. Email secretary@edible.gg to find out more.
