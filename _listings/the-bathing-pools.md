The Bathing Pools is a vibrant destination where everyone can feel welcome and valued. A place where people’s lives are improved. A venue to support the physical, mental and emotional health and wellbeing of our community and visitors to the island. Focusing on education, the history and heritage of the site, sustainability, the environment and social interaction. The year-round, indoor café with an affordable menu, provides a welcoming community space.
