Contract bridge sessions are run each week by several clubs listed on the Channel Islands Contract Bridge Association (Guernsey) website. Clubs include Compass Club (Tuesday evenings), Lihou Bridge Club (Thursday afternoons) and Manor Bridge Club (Friday evenings), plus Thursday evening pairs at Les Cotils. CICBA Guernsey also runs county competitions and the annual Guernsey Bridge Congress, which includes Seniors Pairs.
