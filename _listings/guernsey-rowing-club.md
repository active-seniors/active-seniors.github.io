The Guernsey Rowing Club is one of Guernsey's most successful inter-insular sports clubs and is a vibrant social community. The Club runs a full local race calendar throughout the "summer" and GRC members regularly take part in offshore and coastal regattas held at nearby  clubs. ​See our website for more details.
