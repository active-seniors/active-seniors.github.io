Parkinson's Guernsey runs a weekly programme for people living with Parkinson's, their carers and loved ones. Membership is £25 a year and gives free access to every class Parkinson's Guernsey runs or commissions (marked PG below). Community classes (\*) are free to everyone, and donation-based classes (\*\*) welcome a donation on the day but don't require one. Start with a meet and greet: email info@parkinsons.org.gg. To register, contact Mélanie at melanie@parkinsons.org.gg.

**Mondays**

- 10:30-12:00 Talk of the Month (PG), first Monday of the month, KGV Spensley Room
- 10:30-12:30 Social coffee morning (PG), weekly from the second Monday of the month, KGV Spensley Room
- 10:30-12:30 Nail service (PG), monthly (dates in the newsletter or on the website), KGV Spensley Room
- 11:30-12:30 Walk-in clinic, "Check & Chat with Debbie and Vikki" (PG), third Monday of the month, KGV Spensley Room
- 15:00-16:00 PD Champions (PG), weekly, by referral only, Active Health, Beau Sejour

**Tuesdays**

- 10:00-10:45 Aerobic-based dance class (PG), first and third Tuesdays, all abilities, mainly seated, KGV Spensley Room
- 10:00-10:45 Strength, conditioning and coordination class (\*\*), weekly in term time, Amalgamated Boxing Club, St Martin's
- 10:30-12:00 Singing for Health (\*\*), weekly, all abilities, mainly seated, Styx
- 11:00-12:00 Pickleball and table tennis (PG), weekly, Table Tennis Centre, Hougue du Pommier
- 13:00-14:00 Go Mobility Guernsey (\*), weekly in term time, all abilities, can be seated, Beau Sejour (hall downstairs)
- 14:00-16:00 Wheels for You at the karting track (\*), fortnightly (dates in the newsletter or on the website)

**Wednesdays**

- 12:00-13:00 Walking football (\*), weekly, all abilities, Aztech Soccer Centre, Hougue du Pommier
- 13:00-14:00 Non-contact boxing (PG), fortnightly, all abilities, Amalgamated Boxing Club, St Martin's

**Thursdays**

- 10:45-11:30 Exercise class (PG), weekly, all abilities, mainly seated, KGV Spensley Room
- 10:15-11:30 Tai chi (\*\*), weekly, all abilities, Styx

**Fridays**

- 10:00-11:00 Pétanque club (PG), weekly, weather dependent, all abilities, College Field, King's Road, St Peter Port
- 12:00-13:00 Padel tennis (PG), weekly, Tennis Centre, Route des Longcamps
- 14:00-16:00 Art class: Paint and Create (\*\*), weekly, all abilities, seated, KGV Spensley Room

**Sundays**

- 09:30-12:00 Wheels for You at Beaucamps (\*), fortnightly (dates in the newsletter or on the website), Beaucamps High School

PD Champions, boxing, pickleball and padel need booking; the other sessions are turn-up. Free transport (wheelchair accessible) runs from St Martin's and St Peter Port on Mondays to the KGV sessions and on Thursdays to the exercise class.
