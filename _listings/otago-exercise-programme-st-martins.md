The Otago Exercise Programme is a 12-week strength and balance course for older adults, led by Seema Banhatti. The exercises are done seated, standing and walking around the chairs, and people have found them a gentle way to improve strength, balance and coordination. There are two groups at St Martin's Community Centre, on Tuesdays and Thursdays, in a ground-floor hall with step-free access. The current courses are full and finish around Christmas, and the next course is due to start in the first week of February. Email Seema to ask about a place. There's also a Friday group at St Saviour's Community Centre.
