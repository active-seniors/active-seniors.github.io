The Otago Exercise Programme is a strength and balance course for older adults, run in Guernsey for the first time this autumn. Over 12 weekly sessions it aims to improve strength, balance and confidence. Classes are capped at 20 people, so contact Seema before you go to check there's a place, or to ask about the next course.
