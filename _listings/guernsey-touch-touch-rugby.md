Non-contact touch rugby, with a summer league that drew 26 teams across five divisions in 2025, plus beach touch events. Guernsey Touch fields Mixed, Ladies', Men's and Over 35 Men's teams against Jersey, and has run free learn-to-play taster sessions at KGV.
