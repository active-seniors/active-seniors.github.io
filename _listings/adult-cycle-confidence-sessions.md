Cycling sessions with trained instructors for adults, from complete beginners learning to ride to experienced riders who want more confidence on roads. Sessions run off-road at Les Beaucamps High School alongside the Wheels for You inclusive cycling sessions. A limited number of bikes is available, and riders can book one-to-one time or turn up.
