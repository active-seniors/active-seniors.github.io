The Otago Exercise Programme is a 12-week strength and balance course for older adults, led by Seema Banhatti. The exercises are done seated, standing and walking around the chairs, and people have found them a gentle way to improve strength, balance and coordination. This group meets on Fridays at St Saviour's Community Centre, on the ground floor with step-free access. The current course finishes before Christmas and the next is due to start in February. Email Seema to ask about a place. There are also groups at St Martin's Community Centre on Tuesdays and Thursdays.
