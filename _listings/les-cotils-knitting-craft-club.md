Les Cotils Knitting and Craft Club is lead by Liz Williams on the first Wednesday of each month in the large, light meeting room opposite the reception. There is no cost to join or take part, but members buy refreshments from the bar, which can be tea or coffee if wanted. Members bring their own materials and projects to work on and can be anything from knitting, sewing, making bracelets and other crafts. It is a lovely opportunity to do something creative.
