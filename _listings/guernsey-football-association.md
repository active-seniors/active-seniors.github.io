The Guernsey FA was formed in 1893 when affiliating to The Football Association, and celebrated its 125th anniversary during the 2018/19 season. It is responsible for efficiently governing and developing the local game and that responsibility ensures that the Guernsey FA is committed to positively influencing participation for all in football regardless of age, ability, gender, sexual orientation, race, religion and socio-economic status.
