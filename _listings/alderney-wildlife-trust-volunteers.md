The Alderney Wildlife Trust wants volunteers to support its reserves team in maintaining hides, signage, benches and footpaths, and to lead wildlife and heritage walks and tours for visitors and residents. Its new community hub, The Hut at Saye, hosts volunteering sessions and workshops.
