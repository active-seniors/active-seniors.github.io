About Us We look to build physical health, a positive, egoless mindset and the ability to defend yourself through the art of Brazilian jiu-jitsu. And we have fun at the same time. Brazilian jiu-jitsu is a grappling-based martial art that rose to prominence in the early 90s when it was shown to be the most effective of the martial arts. Our emphasis is on learning, an enjoyable training environment and safety.
