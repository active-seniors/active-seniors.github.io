A unique oasis in the heart of St Peter Port, Marina Health & Leisure is perfectly placed to help you to tune out, switch off and enjoy all the benefits that relaxation can have on your mind and body. Whether you have just enjoyed a gym-based workout, relaxing dip, or an invigorating spell in the sauna or steam room.  With dedicated wet and dry areas both featuring complimentary chilled water and magazines, you can switch off and relax.
