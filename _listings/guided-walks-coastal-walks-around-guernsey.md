The circular 2 hour walks all start from a bus stop and refreshments nearby 5 different walks each Saturday between April and October 1st Saturday L’eree : L’Eree Bay Hotel bus stop 2nd Saturday Vazon : Vistas Café bus stop 3rd Saturday Pleinmont : Imperial Hotel bus stop 4th Saturday Pembroke : Pembroke bus stop In May & August there are 5 Saturdays 5th Saturday Jerbourg : Hotel Jerbourg bus stop These walks may involve rough ground and slopes
