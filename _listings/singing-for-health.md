A relaxed singing session at the Styx Community Centre. Songs are taught by ear, so there's no need to read music and no previous singing experience is needed.

These Bailiwick Social Prescribing community classes are safe, relaxed places for anyone who wants to get more involved in the community and do something good for their health and wellbeing. They're drop-in sessions open to the public, so you don't have to book, though you're welcome to email contactbsp@healthimprovement.gg or call 01481 210433 for more information. When a class is nearly full, long-standing members may be asked to move on to other classes to make room for newcomers, and limited places go to Bailiwick Social Prescribing clients first. If you can pay, there's a minimum voluntary donation of £5, which helps fund more community classes around the island.
