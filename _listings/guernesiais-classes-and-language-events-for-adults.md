The Guernsey Language Commission organises courses, workshops and public events for adults learning Guernésiais, the island's native language. A free taster session for a new adult course was held at the Styx Centre in late 2025, and a February 2026 public event at St Martin's Community Centre drew about 200 islanders and introduced the new adult course. The Commission also points learners to informal conversation sessions such as Speed Patois.
