As well as being a traditional Community Centre and affordable venue for all, Styx Centre believes in standing up for a happy and healthy Community. The aim of Styx Centre's Community Classes & Courses is for anyone to be able to attend and enjoy an activity that may help to improve their health and wellbeing, in a fun and relaxed way.
