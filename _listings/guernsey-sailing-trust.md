The Guernsey Sailing Trust is a charity founded in 1988 to make sailing accessible to all. For adults, it runs courses and private lessons, arranged around availability, in three types of boat: dinghies, which are physically demanding; Hawk20 keelboats, which are gentler; and an RS Venture Connect, a wheelchair-accessible sailing boat. Get in touch to find out what's available.

The Trust also runs the RYA Sailability programme, which opens up sailing and powerboating to people with additional needs, with sponsorship available for those taking part. It offers practical powerboat courses and shore-based courses such as first aid, navigation and sea survival too. During term time it works with primary and secondary schools, and in the school holidays it runs RYA courses for children.
