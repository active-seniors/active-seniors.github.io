Indoor bowls at the six-rink Guernsey Bowls Stadium at Hougue du Pommier, which opens every morning and evening from October to April. The club runs leagues and has social, winter playing and temporary membership categories, and visitors can hire bowls and shoes. There is a bar in the evenings.
