The Guernsey Sailing Trust was established in 1988 to enable local children from all walks of life to experience and enjoy the fun and challenges involved with sailing, as well as to make the sport accessible to all. The Trust also runs the successful RYA Sailability programme for those of all age with any additional needs. During school term time the Sailing Trust is fully occupied in teaching sailing to the Islands Junior and Secondary Schools.
