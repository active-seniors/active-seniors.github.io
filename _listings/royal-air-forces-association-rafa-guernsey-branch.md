The local RAFA branch gives welfare support to RAF veterans and their families, from a conversation to financial help such as mobility equipment. It also takes part in the Battle of Britain parade and the Liberation Day and Remembrance parades. Volunteers help at fundraising events such as the Viaer Marche.
