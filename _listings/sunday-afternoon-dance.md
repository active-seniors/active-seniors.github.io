A monthly afternoon dance at St Martin's Parish Hall, usually on the third Sunday. The afternoon is filled with Ballroom, Latin American, Sequence, Line and fun dances, from 2.30pm to 4.30pm, with a break for tea or coffee, a biscuit or cake, and a chat. A few fun and easy sequence dances mean you can join in at least a couple even without a partner. It's lively: expect to be on your feet all afternoon.
