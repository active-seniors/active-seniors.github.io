The monthly dance takes place at the St Martin's Parish Hall, usually on the third Sunday of every month. The afternoon is filled with all the Ballroom, Latin American, Sequence, Line and Fun dances you can manage. We dance from 2.30pm – 4.30pm with a break in the middle for tea and a biscuit/cake and the chance to chat. We always include a few fun dances and easy sequence dances so that if you turn up without a partner, you will be able to join in.
