Grow is a Guernsey charity, and there are lots of ways to volunteer with it, whatever time you have. You could give an hour a year helping on a flag day, volunteer regularly on site, or share your expertise at director level. Most volunteering takes place at Grow's site at Le Coutanchez, where its crew of adults with learning disabilities work in the Old Potting Shed shop, the café and the workshop, building bird boxes, bug hotels and planters. Volunteering is free. Email hello@grow.gg or call 01481 711600 to find out more.
