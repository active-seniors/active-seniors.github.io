Located in the Regency and Marina Court, Marina Health & Leisure is a high end, adult-only, private members' health and fitness club. The club comprises of a 15m swimming pool, a rooftop tennis court, and a fully equipped state-of-the-art gym. The gym, changing rooms and reception areas have been fully redecorated throughout with many new features. The club also has its very own, all-weather private rooftop tennis court.
