This is an outdoor drawing club that meets weekly from mid-April to September at different locations around St Peter Port to sketch the town's buildings, streets and squares. It is open to all abilities and needs no art experience. The organiser gives a short talk on each location, and the year's work is exhibited at the George Crossan Gallery.
