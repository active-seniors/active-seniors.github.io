Rovers is a multi sport community club based near the coast at Port Soif in Guernsey. We occupy an 11 acre site enclosed within the 1,143m long Port Soif nature trail. 7 acres are maintained for sports use be that football, cricket, touch rugby or archery. Our facilities are amongst the best the island has to offer. The clubhouse opened in 1996 and was extended in 2008. We continue to invest in our facilities for the benefit of our members and the wider sporting public.
