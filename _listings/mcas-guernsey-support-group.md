A safe, friendly space for people living with Mast Cell Activation Syndrome (MCAS) in Guernsey. This group is for anyone who has been diagnosed with MCAS, suspects they may have MCAS, or is supporting a family member or loved one with the condition. Living with MCAS can sometimes feel isolating, especially when it feels like you’re the only person dealing with it. This group is to connect people locally so we can share experiences, support and hopefully build a community where nobody feels alone.
