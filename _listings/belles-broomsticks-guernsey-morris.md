Morris dancing for the love of dance and music, around the Bailiwick and beyond. The group is always looking for new members, musicians and budding dancers who want to try something a bit different. A good sense of fun is essential; it's an aerobic workout, good for the mind, and comes with a great social life. Get in touch before you go to check the venue and time.
