An annual festival, running for 96 years in 2026, where people of all ages can compete, perform or exhibit. Disciplines include music, speech and drama, Guernesiais, arts and crafts, photography, literary work and cookery. The 2026 festival ran from February to 25 April and included adult Guernesiais classes, an exhibition at Beau Sejour and a gala concert.
