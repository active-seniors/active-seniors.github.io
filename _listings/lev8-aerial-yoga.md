Aerial yoga is playful and creates a feeling of serenity. The support of the sling allows us to experience poses we may not be able to achieve on the mat and allows for a deeper stretch. Classes are a place to restore, release and reinvigorate. It’s never too late for a new love in your life! There's also a Slow and Low class, a more relaxing and restorative option. Classes cost £15 an hour; contact Lottie Evans by email.
