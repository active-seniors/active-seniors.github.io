The Art of Living is a non-profit organisation founded in 1981 by the world-renowned humanitarian and spiritual leader Sri Sri Ravi Shankar. Art of Living offers effective educational and self-development programs and tools that facilitate the elimination of stress and foster deep and profound inner peace, happiness and well-being. These programs, which include breathing techniques, meditation, yoga, and practical wisdom for daily living.
