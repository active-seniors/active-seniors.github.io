The aim is simple: athletics doesn't have to stop at any particular age, and there's real value - physically and otherwise - in staying active and competitive throughout life. Whether you competed years ago and haven't been back since, or you've never tried track and field at all, we stage events and occasional training sessions that are a low-pressure way to take part.
