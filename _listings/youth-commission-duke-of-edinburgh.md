The operating licence for the Duke of Edinburgh’s Award (DofE) in Guernsey, has now transferred to the States of Guernsey Education Office. - If you have any questions please reach out to them. For all young people who are home-educated or not attending Duke of Edinburgh through their school, they are able to do their Residential independently with The Youth Commission. If you are aged between 14 and 24 years old you could start working towards your Duke of Edinburgh’s award. Please reach out.
