Our qualified guides share a passion for the Islands of Guernsey introducing the history and people, revealing folklore and legends in an informative and entertaining style. Guided tours cover a variety of different subject areas and special interests. These can include the other Bailiwick islands, Lihou, Herm, Sark, Alderney; walking in the footsteps of famous visitors such as Victor Hugo and Renoir; discovering more about the island’s five year long occupation.
