Guernsey Paddle Club (GPC) is principally aimed at standup paddle boarding and welcomes all abilities and levels. They hold races once a month and there is healthy happy social element to these get togethers. GPC welcomes all abilities but expects participants to be able to stand and paddle independently, we are not a school. There is a wide range of levels that participate. They can help with boards if new comers want to try but don't have boards. There are also other social events like the Santa Paddle at Christmas and the end of summer Series prize giving and social event.
