Guardian Medical has an extensive selection of first aid kits, from compact travel packs and products ideal for the home to larger first aid boxes suitable for the workplace.  We also supply hand gels, sanitising wipes and other cleaning products. Our friendly staff will be happy to advise you about finding a first aid kit to suit your needs. We are trained to visit people in residential homes or private residences to help identify hazards and find simple solutions to everyday problems.
