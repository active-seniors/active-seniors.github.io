This is a monthly series of illustrated evening talks on local history, family and local fiction topics. The talks are given by an accredited Guernsey guide from Guernsey Walking Tours. The 2025-26 season ran on the second Tuesday of each month from October 2025 to April 2026, and tea and coffee were served afterwards.
