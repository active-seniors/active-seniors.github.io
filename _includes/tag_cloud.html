{% comment %} Tag cloud for one dimension of a listing's vocabulary — either its activity-type `tags` or its need/context `suitable_for` values. The two are deliberately kept as separate dimensions (interests vs. needs), not one mixed cloud — see planning/landing-page-ux.md.
